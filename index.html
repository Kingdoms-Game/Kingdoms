📜 GAME RULES 📜

OBJECTIVE: Defeat your opponent through 3 escalating phases (Jack ➔ Queen ➔ King) by managing your hand and executing successful attacks.

TURNS & COMBAT:

  • Attacker plays cards into the battlefield first, which may add up to a maximum of 10. However, during any "King Round," the max increases to 20.
  • Defender responds by playing cards to match or beat the attack total. Overkill is -not- rewarded.

OUTCOMES:

  • Blocked: Defender's total $\ge$ Attack total. Roles swap and play continues.
  • Success: Attack exceeds defense by the phase threshold (Jack: 1, Queen: 2, King: 3).
  • Forfeit: Defender can pass to concede the round and take the damage.

SPECIAL RULES:

  • Aces (A): Are Wild (use arrows to adjust the number)
  • 2 vs 10 Counter: A 2 directly blocks and nullifies an opposing 10.
