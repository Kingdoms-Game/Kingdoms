🛡️ BLOCKED!
📜 GAME RULES 📜

OBJECTIVE: Defeat your opponent through 3 escalating phases (Jack ➔ Queen ➔ King).

TURNS:

  • Attacker plays cards into battlefield first.
  • Defender responds to match or beat attack total.

OUTCOMES:

  • Blocked: Defender's total $\ge$ Attack. Roles swap.
  • Success: Attack exceeds defense by threshold (Jack: 1, Queen: 2, King: 3). Defender loses phase & HP.

SPECIALS:

  • Aces (A): Wild values from A up to 10.
  • 2 vs 10: A 2 directly cancels out an opposing 10.
