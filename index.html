📜 GAME RULES 📜

OBJECTIVE: Each Kingdom has 3 Royals: Jack (1 hp) ➔ Queen (2 hp) ➔ KING (3 hp).

  • Defeat the Enemy Kingdom's Royal Family!

    COMBAT:

    • Players wield 9 cards and an Ace (wild), trying to hurl larger numbers at the other.
    • the ATTACKER plays cards into the Battlefield first

      (to a max of 10 or 20).

    • DEFENDER must match or exceed the attack total

      (overkill is -not- rewarded).

    • during KING ROUNDS, players play with 18 cards each and the Max doubles.

    OUTCOMES:

    • Success: Attack exceeds Defense's number enough to destroy the Royal.
    • Blocked: Defender equals or exceeds the Attacker's number. ROLES SWAP and play continues.
    • Forfeit: Defender can pass to concede the round.

    SPECIAL RULES:

    • Aces: are Wild (can be adjusted when selected).
    • David & Goliath: A 2 directly blocks and nullifies an opposing 10.
